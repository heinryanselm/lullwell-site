Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Lullwell helps both parents look after a new baby and the mother's recovery. That means it holds sensitive information about your family's health. We collect as little as we can, keep most of it on your phone, and never sell it, share it with advertisers or use it to track you.

The short version
  • Everything you log is stored on your phone.
  • If you link phones with a partner, the shared logs are also stored on our server so both phones stay in step.
  • If you choose to use Nell, the AI helper, your question and the relevant logs are sent to our AI provider to write the answer.
  • Mood check-ins never leave your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: Help → Delete all my data.

Health data and our legal basis

Information about pregnancy, birth, recovery, medicines and your baby's health is "special category" data under UK GDPR and EU GDPR. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give when you set up the app, link phones or turn on Nell. You can withdraw consent at any time by stopping sharing, not using Nell, or deleting your data. We process account and subscription data because it's needed to provide the service you asked for (contract). We send tips and offers by email only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Data on our server stays while your family uses linked phones. When you choose Delete all my data, your account is deleted straight away. If you were the last person in the family, all of the family's records are deleted too. If your partner is still linked, the shared records stay available to them, because they belong to their family log as well. Backups are overwritten within 7 days.

Children

Lullwell is for parents and carers aged 18 or over. It holds information about babies only as entered by their parents. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on our server. Row-level security means a phone can only ever read its own family's records. Linking needs a one-time code that only you can share.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
