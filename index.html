Questions

Is Lullwell medical advice?

No. It gives general information from official guidance and doesn’t replace your midwife, health visitor or GP. In an emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number.

How do we share it?

Set it up on one phone, then invite your partner from the Family screen. Once their phone joins, you both see the same day as it happens. One subscription covers you both.

Who is Nell?

Nell is the AI helper inside Lullwell. She answers questions using your own logs and official guidance, and tells you when to call someone. She only switches on if you agree, and your conversations stay on your phone.

Does it work after a C-section, or with twins?

Yes. Recovery advice and reminders change with the type of birth, and twins, triplets or an older sibling each get their own log.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Lullwell is on iPhone first, and Android is coming.
