Questions

Is Lullwell medical advice?

No. It gives general information from official guidance (the NHS in the UK, the CDC, AAP and ACOG in the US, and the WHO elsewhere) and doesn’t replace your midwife, health visitor or GP. In an emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number.

How do my partner and I share it?

Set it up on one phone, then invite your partner from the Family screen. Once their phone joins, you both see everything live. One subscription covers you both.

Who is Nell?

Nell is Lullwell’s AI helper. She answers questions using your own logs and official guidance, and tells you when it’s time to call someone. She only switches on if you agree, and your conversations stay on your phone.

Does it work after a C-section, or with twins?

Yes. Recovery advice and reminders change with the type of birth, and twins, triplets or an older sibling each get their own log.

What happens to my data?

Your logs stay on your phone unless you link with your partner. Mood check-ins never leave the phone. No ads, no tracking, and you can delete everything from inside the app. See the privacy policy.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Lullwell is on iPhone first, and Android is on the way.
