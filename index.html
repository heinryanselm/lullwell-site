Questions

Good to know

Is Lullwell medical advice?

No. Lullwell gives general information from official guidance (the NHS in the UK, the CDC, AAP and ACOG in the US, and the World Health Organization elsewhere), and it doesn’t replace your midwife, health visitor or GP. In an emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number.

How do my partner and I share it?

Set up Lullwell on one phone, then invite your partner from the Family screen. Once their phone joins, every feed, nappy, dose and nap syncs live between you. One subscription covers you both.

Who is Nell?

Nell is Lullwell’s AI helper. She answers questions using your own logs and official guidance, and tells you when it’s time to call someone. She only switches on when you agree, and your conversations stay on your phone.

Does it work for C-sections and twins?

Yes. Recovery advice, reminders and warning signs change with the type of birth, and twins, triplets or an older sibling each get their own log.

What happens to my data?

Your logs stay on your phone unless you link phones with your partner, in which case they’re stored securely so both phones can see them. Mood check-ins never leave your phone. There are no ads and no tracking, and you can delete everything from inside the app. Read the privacy policy.

Is it on Android?

Lullwell is on iPhone first. Android is on the way.
