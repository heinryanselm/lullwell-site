Common questions

How do I link my partner's phone? Tap the family button at the top of Today, then Invite my partner to send them a six-character code. On their phone, they install Lullwell, open the same screen, tap I have a code and type it in.

Reminders aren't arriving. Check that notifications are allowed for Lullwell in your phone's Settings. On Android, also allow "Alarms and reminders" so doses arrive on time.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lullwell. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. One subscription covers your whole linked family.

How do I delete my data? In the app: Help → Delete all my data. This deletes everything on your phone, plus your account and synced data on our server. See the privacy policy for details.
